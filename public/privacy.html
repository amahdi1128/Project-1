Privacy notice

Updated November 19, 2026 · Pilot research only

BuildPulse is an experimental construction-data dashboard, independent of the City of Chicago. The public permit explorer reads public municipal records and does not require you to create an account or submit an email address.

Early-access research

When signup is enabled and you voluntarily submit the research form, BuildPulse records your work email, selected trade, optional company name, indicated monthly price range, whether you are interested in a pilot, your consent, and the date of submission. This information is used only to assess demand, contact you about the product research or early-access pilot, and answer related requests. We do not collect payment details through this form.

Research submissions are stored in a private database, not in publicly downloadable permit files. We do not sell these submissions. Access is limited to the BuildPulse operator and technical providers needed to operate the service. We retain information only as needed for the pilot research and delete it on request or when it is no longer needed.

Opting out and deleting information

You can opt out of research outreach by replying to any BuildPulse email and requesting removal. Because the project is not yet collecting submissions, a dedicated private support address is still being established. Until a contact channel and secure database are activated, the form remains disabled.

Technical operation

The site uses hosting infrastructure to serve HTML, JavaScript and municipal data. Standard hosting logs may include IP addresses and browser metadata for security and service operations. The permit browser does not require advertising cookies or third-party analytics.

Public permit information

Records shown in the dashboard originate from the City of Chicago's building permits dataset. Reported values and dates may be changed by the city. BuildPulse is not affiliated with the city.
