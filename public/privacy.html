Privacy Policy and Notice at Collection

Effective: November 20, 2026. Operator: BuildThePulse, an early-stage independent project. Site: buildthepulse.onrender.com.

Important: This policy describes the current prototype; it is not a representation that BuildThePulse meets every law that could apply to a future commercial service. Applicability of CCPA/CPRA, California Invasion of Privacy Act (CIPA), Delaware Personal Data Privacy Act, Colorado Privacy Act, Virginia CDPA, Connecticut Data Privacy Act, Canadian privacy laws, and other rules depends on facts including business size, jurisdiction, users, and actual data processing. Review this notice and the implementation with qualified counsel as advertising or other tracking systems evolve.

1. Information we collect and the purposes

Public permit data: The public dashboard republishes selected construction-permit information from government sources (permit identifiers, publicly reported project locations, descriptions, costs when available, dates or years, and market categories). We omit dedicated applicant and contact fields and filter recognizable email addresses and phone numbers from project descriptions. Published locations may include residential addresses or parcel identifiers. Free-text source records can contain incidental information. Use the form below to ask us to review or correct a record on this site. Our uses are public construction-market research, search, filtering, and data export.

Information you voluntarily submit: If you join the free-updates interest list, we collect your work email, trade, optional company name, contact consent and submission timestamp so we can notify you about the planned free industry digest; no automated digest is currently sent. Existing stored research records may also contain historical price-interest or pilot-interest fields from our earlier prototype. If you request sponsorship information, we collect company and contact names, work email, optional business website, market, sponsorship format, estimated budget, notes, consent and timestamp. If you submit a privacy-rights request, we collect the email address, request category, optional jurisdiction and details, and processing status. We do not currently collect payment-card details or operate checkout.

Technical and aggregate analytics data: Render and Supabase may process IP address, request time, browser/user-agent details and technical security/error logs to deliver and protect the site. With your opt-in consent (and absent a conflicting Global Privacy Control preference), our first-party analytics function records only event type (page view, market selection, or sponsor click), page category, market, optional sponsor placement and timestamp. It does not store email addresses, IP addresses, visitor IDs, referrer URLs or browser fingerprints in its analytics table. Because events have no visitor identifier, statistics cannot reliably report unique visitors and individual aggregate events cannot be attributed back to a person for deletion. We currently have no third-party advertising pixel, Google AdSense tag, session replay or cross-site audience profiling installed.

Information sources: You directly, public government permit feeds, and standard service infrastructure. We do not knowingly buy personal profiles from data brokers.

2. Use, disclosure and retention

Information is used to operate the free public data explorer; maintain security, fix errors and prevent abuse; count consented aggregate audience events to understand product use and support sponsorship discussions; evaluate sponsor inquiries; receive and respond to privacy requests; and, when explicitly opted into, contact readers about free updates. We do not currently sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Clearly labeled contextual sponsors may pay for placement, but do not receive private subscriber lists. We do not use sensitive personal information for profiling or automated decisions with significant legal effects.

Public permit data is retained and refreshed as needed for the dashboard. Free-updates opt-ins and sponsor inquiries are retained while relevant to the stated purpose or until an appropriate deletion request is fulfilled, subject to lawful needs. Aggregate event counts may be retained for measuring performance, but the underlying events contain no visitor identifier. We are implementing a more defined retention schedule before broad commercial advertising. Privacy requests and related records are kept as reasonably necessary to complete requests, comply with applicable law, and demonstrate compliance. Host logs and backups follow the applicable provider's retention schedules, which may vary. We will adopt more precise retention limits before scaling commercial operations.

We may disclose necessary data to service providers solely to operate the application, process sponsor and subscriber inquiries and maintain security, or when lawfully compelled. We do not post subscriber or sponsor submissions or privacy requests in the public GitHub repository.

3. Cookies, local storage, and CIPA-related technologies

Current deployment: Optional first-party, aggregate audience analytics runs only after you choose to allow it. The site stores the preference in your browser using local storage (buildthepulse_privacy_choice_v3) and sends pageview, market-selection and sponsor-click counts through our Supabase function; it does not create persistent analytics cookies or unique visitor identifiers. The site uses contextual, clearly labeled sponsor placements which do not require analytics consent. No Google advertising network, third-party analytics pixel, session-recording, call-recording or chat interception script is currently installed. The public explorer works without an account. Infrastructure providers may use technical security or delivery technologies outside our application code.

Before we introduce Google AdSense or other third-party advertising cookies, pixels, replay SDKs, cross-site tracking, or communication-monitoring tools, we will update this notice, conduct an applicable consent and CIPA/wiretapping-law review, and implement actual pre-consent blocking where required. We do not treat merely displaying this banner as permission to intercept visitor communications.

Use Your Privacy Choices to reject optional technologies or adjust your locally stored preference. The site honors supported Global Privacy Control signals by refusing optional tracking; Do Not Track is also treated conservatively as an opt-out. Browser or device settings can be used to delete stored preferences, block cookies or clear site data. The consent control can be reopened from the footer.

4. Service providers, transfers and security

GitHub provides code and publicly published municipal-data hosting; Render hosts the static website; Supabase hosts restricted databases and server-side submission functions. We use these providers for infrastructure, not for selling customer data. They may process technical information and transfer it internationally under their own terms. The current project uses U.S.-region Supabase hosting, and service access may occur across borders. Some externally linked government sites have independent privacy and cookie practices that we do not control.

The site submits free-updates signups, sponsorship inquiries, audience events and privacy requests through server-side functions. We use HTTPS and do not put privileged database keys in browser code. No system can guarantee perfect security; suspected incidents will be investigated and notifications issued as required by applicable law.

5. Consumer rights — California and other jurisdictions

Depending on your residency and whether the relevant law applies to this project, you may have rights to know/access information, obtain copies, correct inaccuracies, delete information, opt out of sale or sharing/targeted advertising, limit certain sensitive-data uses, withdraw consent, appeal certain decisions, and be free from unlawful discrimination for exercising rights. California privacy laws may also require disclosure of categories collected, sources, purposes, retention and recipient categories, and recognition of opt-out preference signals. We currently do not sell or share information for targeted advertising, so no such processing is presently conducted.

California notice at collection: Before collecting free-update or sponsor data, the relevant form describes the categories requested, purpose and contact consent. Categories include identifiers (work email, company or contact name and website), professional interests (trade and market), commercial interests (proposed sponsor format and budget), and communications (sponsor messages and rights requests). Optional, consented aggregate analytics captures event type and market without a stored visitor identifier; hosting logs may contain internet/network identifiers. We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information for commercial profiling. Retention follows Section 2.

Global Privacy Control and “Do Not Sell or Share”: Where an applicable law grants an opt-out, supported GPC signals are respected and also prevent our optional audience analytics. You may request an opt-out through the form below, even though BuildThePulse does not currently sell or share personal information. We will not discriminate for exercising applicable privacy rights. We may need to verify your identity before releasing or deleting information; do not send passwords, government IDs, or other sensitive records through this form.

Other U.S. states and Canada: We intend to evaluate requests under the relevant applicable law, including the Delaware Personal Data Privacy Act and comprehensive state privacy statutes where relevant. If Canadian users or commercial operations are introduced, we will separately review PIPEDA, provincial privacy statutes and cross-border rules before collecting additional user data.

6. Submit a privacy rights request

You may submit a request to access, correct or delete data, opt out, or raise another privacy concern using this private request form. It is not a public comment board. We will assess the request, verify identity when needed, and respond in accordance with applicable timelines. An automated on-screen acknowledgment only means the request was stored, not that identity verification or substantive processing has been completed.

To report a permit as displayed here, choose “Other inquiry, appeal, or permit correction” and include the market and permit number. For corrections to the original record, contact the issuing agency.

7. Children, changes and contact

The product is intended for business users, not children under 16. We do not knowingly solicit information from children. If we learn we received such a submission, we will investigate and delete it as appropriate.

We may revise this notice as the product evolves. Material changes, especially involving new third-party advertising tags, recording technology or sensitive-data processing, will be described before deployment and may require fresh consent. The update date will appear at the top of this page.

Privacy contact: Use the secure request form above. A publicly listed business contact mailbox is not yet established; until then, submitted requests must be reviewed by the site operator in the restricted Supabase database.
