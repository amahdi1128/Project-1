Privacy Policy and Notice at Collection

Effective: November 19, 2026. Operator: BuildThePulse, an early-stage independent project. Site: buildthepulse.onrender.com.

Important: This policy describes the current prototype; it is not a representation that BuildThePulse meets every law that could apply to a future commercial service. Applicability of CCPA/CPRA, California Invasion of Privacy Act (CIPA), Delaware Personal Data Privacy Act, Colorado Privacy Act, Virginia CDPA, Connecticut Data Privacy Act, Canadian privacy laws, and other rules depends on facts including business size, jurisdiction, users, and actual data processing. Review this notice and the implementation with qualified counsel before launching paid offerings or tracking technology.

1. Information we collect and the purposes

Public permit data: The public dashboard republishes selected construction-permit information from government sources (permit identifiers, publicly reported project locations, descriptions, costs when available, dates or years, and market categories). It intentionally excludes applicant names, applicant email addresses and phone numbers from its collected feeds. Some published project locations may nonetheless relate to residences. Our uses are public construction-market research, search, filtering, and data export.

Information you voluntarily submit: If you sign up for the research waitlist when enabled, we collect the work email, trade, optional company name, indicated price range, paid-pilot interest, contact consent and submission timestamp. If you submit a privacy request, we collect the email address and request category, optional jurisdiction and details, and a processing status. These are used to administer those requests, respond to them, and assess product demand. No credit-card details are currently collected and no paid checkout is live.

Technical data: Render and other infrastructure providers may process IP address, request time, browser/user-agent details and security/error logs to deliver and protect the site. We do not currently deploy our own behavioral analytics, advertising pixels, fingerprinting, chat widgets, session replay, or cross-site profiling.

Information sources: You directly, public government permit feeds, and standard service infrastructure. We do not knowingly buy personal profiles from data brokers.

2. Use, disclosure and retention

Information is used to operate the public data explorer; maintain security, fix errors and prevent abuse; receive and respond to privacy requests; and, when separately opted into, contact interested prospective customers about research or early access. We do not currently sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use sensitive personal information for profiling or automated decisions with significant legal effects.

Public permit data is retained and refreshed as needed for the dashboard. Research submissions are retained for the duration of pilot validation or until a deletion request is fulfilled, subject to lawful needs. Privacy requests and related records are kept as reasonably necessary to complete requests, comply with applicable law, and demonstrate compliance. Host logs and backups follow the applicable provider's retention schedules, which may vary. We will adopt more precise retention limits before scaling commercial operations.

We may disclose necessary data to service providers solely to operate the application, process submissions and maintain security, or when lawfully compelled. We do not post research submissions or privacy requests in the public GitHub repository.

3. Cookies, local storage, and CIPA-related technologies

Current deployment: No optional marketing, third-party analytics, behavioral advertising, session recording, call recording or live-chat interception SDKs are installed by BuildThePulse. The site uses a small first-party local-storage record (`buildthepulse_privacy_choice_v1`) to remember cookie preferences, including whether optional technology would be allowed if later offered. The public data explorer works without an account. Host infrastructure may use necessary security or delivery technologies outside our application code.

Before we introduce any nonessential cookies, pixels, replay SDKs, cross-site tracking, or communication-monitoring tools, we will update this notice, conduct an applicable consent and CIPA/wiretapping-law review, and implement actual pre-consent blocking where required. We do not treat merely displaying this banner as permission to intercept visitor communications.

Use Your Privacy Choices to reject optional technologies or adjust your locally stored preference. The site honors supported Global Privacy Control signals by refusing optional tracking; Do Not Track is also treated conservatively as an opt-out. Browser or device settings can be used to delete stored preferences, block cookies or clear site data. The consent control can be reopened from the footer.

4. Service providers, transfers and security

GitHub provides code and publicly published municipal-data hosting; Render hosts the static website; Supabase hosts restricted databases and server-side submission functions. We use these providers for infrastructure, not for selling customer data. They may process technical information and transfer it internationally under their own terms. The current project uses U.S.-region Supabase hosting, and service access may occur across borders. Some externally linked government sites have independent privacy and cookie practices that we do not control.

Database tables for waitlist and rights requests have row-level security enabled, no public read access, and restricted server-side writes. We use HTTPS and do not put privileged database keys in browser code. No system can guarantee perfect security; suspected incidents will be investigated and notifications issued as required by applicable law.

5. Consumer rights — California and other jurisdictions

Depending on your residency and whether the relevant law applies to this project, you may have rights to know/access information, obtain copies, correct inaccuracies, delete information, opt out of sale or sharing/targeted advertising, limit certain sensitive-data uses, withdraw consent, appeal certain decisions, and be free from unlawful discrimination for exercising rights. California privacy laws may also require disclosure of categories collected, sources, purposes, retention and recipient categories, and recognition of opt-out preference signals. We currently do not sell or share information for targeted advertising, so no such processing is presently conducted.

California notice at collection: Before collecting waitlist data, the form describes the information requested, its purpose and consent. The categories are identifiers (work email, optional organization); professional interests (trade); commercial preferences (stated price interest); and communications/requests (pilot interest and privacy inquiries). Hosting logs may contain internet/network activity identifiers. We currently do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information for commercial profiling. Information is retained as described in Section 2, not for an unlimited period.

Global Privacy Control and “Do Not Sell or Share”: Where an applicable law grants an opt-out, supported GPC signals are respected. You may request an opt-out through the form below, even though BuildThePulse does not currently sell or share personal information. We will not discriminate for exercising applicable privacy rights. We may need to verify your identity before releasing or deleting information; do not send passwords, government IDs, or other sensitive records through this form.

Other U.S. states and Canada: We intend to evaluate requests under the relevant applicable law, including the Delaware Personal Data Privacy Act and comprehensive state privacy statutes where relevant. If Canadian users or commercial operations are introduced, we will separately review PIPEDA, provincial privacy statutes and cross-border rules before collecting additional user data.

6. Submit a privacy rights request

You may submit a request to access, correct or delete data, opt out, or raise another privacy concern using this private request form. It is not a public comment board. We will assess the request, verify identity when needed, and respond in accordance with applicable timelines. An automated on-screen acknowledgment only means the request was stored, not that identity verification or substantive processing has been completed.

For concerns about public permit-record accuracy, please use the corresponding municipal data source; BuildThePulse does not issue or amend government permits.

7. Children, changes and contact

The product is intended for business users, not children under 16. We do not knowingly solicit information from children. If we learn we received such a submission, we will investigate and delete it as appropriate.

We may revise this notice as the product evolves. Material changes, especially involving new analytics, advertising, recording or sensitive-data processing, will be described before deployment and may require new consent. The update date will appear at the top of this page.

Privacy contact: Use the secure request form above. A publicly listed business contact mailbox is not yet established; until then, submitted requests must be reviewed by the site operator in the restricted Supabase database.
