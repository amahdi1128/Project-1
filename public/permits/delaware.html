Explore Delaware permits

What the feed covers

Delaware has two sources in the explorer. Sussex County supplies recent records with individual issue dates and parcel references when available. The Delaware Office of State Planning Coordination supplies statewide permit-location records with a reporting year.

How to find permits

Look at the date column to distinguish recent Sussex County permits from annual statewide records. Annual records are labeled “year only” and may list a jurisdiction or county in place of a street address.

Coverage limits and official sources

Sussex County’s recent feed does not cover every Delaware municipality. Annual statewide records describe longer-term trends and do not establish a day of issue or an open construction opportunity.
