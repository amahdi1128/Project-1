Explore Washington, D.C. permits

What the feed covers

The District of Columbia Department of Buildings publishes a rolling feed of building permits issued in the last 30 days. The dashboard includes the permit ID, issue date, address, ward, and available descriptions of the work.

How to find permits

Select a ward to focus on one part of the District, or search by street and work description. Export the filtered records if you want to compare permit activity by ward or project type.

Coverage limits and official sources

BuildThePulse leaves project costs blank for this feed. For records outside the rolling window or authoritative details on a permit, consult the District’s source.
