Explore Philadelphia permits

What the feed covers

The explorer uses Philadelphia Department of Licenses and Inspections permit records with recent issue dates. Records can include the permit number, site address, permit type, approved scope of work, and council district.

How to find permits

Paste a permit number to find a particular record, or search an address or phrase from the approved scope. Use the permit type and work description to tell building work apart from other covered permit activity.

Coverage limits and official sources

The dashboard does not supply Philadelphia project valuations. Use the official source for older records and to confirm the scope of a specific permit.
