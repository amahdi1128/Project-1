Explore Chicago permits

What the feed covers

The City of Chicago feed shows permits with issue dates in a rolling window of about 30 days. Entries can include the permit number, street address, ward, work description, and applicant-reported project cost.

How to find permits

Enter part of an address or a keyword such as “roof” or “electrical.” Use the ward filter to narrow the results or set a minimum reported value when comparing projects with cost data.

Coverage limits and official sources

Project cost is self-reported and may be missing. For older permits or the current status of a specific permit, check the city’s full dataset using the permit number.
